    How This AI Works Without Machine Learning

    What is Symbolic AI (GOFAI)?

    In Artificial Intelligence, Symbolic AI (Good Old-Fashioned AI) represents knowledge through explicit rules, ontologies, and logical inference rather than probabilistic neural networks. Expert systems, chess engines (like Deep Blue), and grammar checkers are classic examples of symbolic AI.

    Key Algorithmic Components Used:

    1. Deterministic Tokenizer & Porter Stemmer: Normalizes candidate speech by reducing words to morphological roots (e.g. "encapsulating" → "encapsul") without neural embeddings.
    2. Knowledge Ontology & Concept Coverage Engine: Evaluates answers against hierarchical concept trees with weighted technical synonyms and bounded Levenshtein edit distance for typo tolerance.
    3. STAR Behavioral Finite State Grammar: Regular expression automata that identify transitional linguistic markers for Situation, Task, Action, and Result in behavioral narratives.
    4. Prosody & Delivery Telemetry: Real-time Words-Per-Minute (WPM) calculation against an optimal 115-160 WPM cadence, plus regex-based filler word density analysis.
    5. Lexicon-Based Assertiveness & Tone Analysis: Symbolic polarity scoring that rewards proactive engineering verbs while penalizing tentative hedging language.

    Advantages Over Machine Learning:

    • 100% Explainable: Candidates and interviewers see the exact rules triggered with zero black-box obscurity.
    • Zero Hallucination: Never fabricates facts or invents invalid feedback.
    • Zero Cloud Costs / Offline: Runs entirely in the client's browser with 0 external API calls.
    • Deterministic & Repeatable: The exact same answer produces the exact same score every time.
